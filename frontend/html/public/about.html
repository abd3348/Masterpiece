WHY CAMPUS MATE

A home is more than an address.

A SIMPLE LISTING MODEL

Find your home. Agree directly.

Landlords pay JD 20 per property advertisement. Tenants browse and connect for free. Rent and deposits are agreed and paid directly between landlord and tenant. Campus Mate displays asking prices and does not handle rental payments.
