Finding a home means balancing your budget, your commute, and the way you like to live. Sharing it means finding someone whose everyday habits work with yours.

Campus Mate connects these decisions. Compare spaces, explore roommate preferences, and keep track of the steps from your first rental request to moving in. The choice stays yours.
