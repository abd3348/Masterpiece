Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers before your next move.

8 questions

Who can use Campus Mate?

Students, employees, interns, expatriates, and anyone exploring housing in Jordan. You can browse publicly; account actions require signing in.

How do I create an account?

Open Register, choose tenant or property owner, and complete the form. In the demo, newly created accounts last only for the current session.

Can I choose my roommate?

Yes. Choose someone on the Roommates page or in the rental request. You can rent alone or select a roommate yourself; compatibility scores do not make that choice for you. Use Book a meeting to request a video introduction; the other person accepts or declines the invitation.

What happens after I request a rental?

The owner reviews the request. After approval, the tenant completes the agreement, inspection, direct arrangement confirmation, and move-in steps. Rent and deposits are agreed and paid directly to the landlord.

Who pays Campus Mate, and how is rent paid?

Landlords pay JD 20 for each new property advertisement. Tenants browse for free and pay rent and deposits directly to their landlord. Campus Mate does not collect or verify rental payments or issue rent receipts. Listing fee payments in this frontend demo are simulated; no money is charged.

Are the AI features connected to a real AI service?

In demo mode, the assistant and compatibility scores use local rules. They help illustrate the interface and do not analyze your photos or videos.

Why do changes disappear after a refresh?

The demo keeps changes in page memory without LocalStorage or SessionStorage. A future connected backend will be responsible for saving account data.

How do I report a listing or ask for help?

Use the report action in the property's details for listing concerns. For general guidance, visit Support.
