Using the project preview

What to expect while exploring Campus Mate.

A frontend demonstration

The listings, compatibility scores, market figures, and demo accounts illustrate the project. Rental arrangements, listing fee confirmations, and support messages in demo mode do not create real transactions.

Your account journey

Your signed-in role determines which pages and actions are available. Tenants choose their roommate and submit requests; owners review requests and manage their properties.

Use sample information

Try the demo with sample account details. Avoid entering passwords used elsewhere or sensitive information in messages and uploads.

Listing fees and direct rent payments

Landlords pay JD 20 for each new property advertisement before publication. Editing the same paid advertisement does not incur another posting fee. The fee does not guarantee a tenant or bypass listing review.

Displayed rent is the landlord's asking price. The landlord and tenant agree the final rent, deposit, lease and payment method directly. Campus Mate does not collect, hold or verify rent or deposits and does not issue rental payment receipts. Listing fee confirmation is simulated in this preview.

Connected operation

A future backend must provide real authentication, authorization, data storage, and landlord listing fee processing before the platform operates as a live service.
